## About me

I am a university professor and researcher at the **Federal Center for Technological Education of Minas Gerais (CEFET-MG)**, Brazil, working at the intersection of **computer science, artificial intelligence, and bioengineering**.

My research interests include machine learning, computational drug discovery, bioinformatics, cheminformatics, molecular modeling, graph-based analysis, and the design of scientific software. I am particularly interested in transforming complex computational methods into accessible, reproducible tools for research and education.

## Featured project

### [BioMolExplorer](https://github.com/mpiress/BioMolExplorer) — Molecular Data Exploration for Intelligent Drug Discovery

An integrated computational framework for computer-aided drug design, bringing together molecular information retrieval, fingerprint-based similarity analysis, graph-based exploration, consensus docking, redocking validation, and ADMET profiling.

**Highlights:** molecular data integration (PDB, ChEMBL, ZINC) · molecular networks · AutoDock Vina / DOCK6 · scientific workflows.

**[Explore the repository →](https://github.com/mpiress/BioMolExplorer)** · **[Documentation and website →](https://mpiress.github.io/BioMolExplorer/)**
